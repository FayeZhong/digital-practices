An introduction

About Me

Hello, I’m Yifei, a Digital Media student at the University of Leeds. My academic background spans media, communication and computing, and I’m interested in bringing these perspectives together through creative practice.

My background

I completed a dual undergraduate degree in Network and New Media and Computing Systems. My studies covered a wide range of subjects, including communication studies, journalism, editing and publishing, advertising, marketing and project management. On the computing side, I studied data analysis, mechatronics, machine learning and artificial intelligence, and website development, among other subjects.

This breadth gave me an introduction to different disciplines and ways of thinking, although there was less opportunity to explore each area in depth. I now want to build on that foundation and develop a more focused understanding of digital media, alongside stronger practical skills.

Returning to web design

In my third year as an undergraduate, I created a website about travelling in Singapore using HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with JSON for data. More than two years later, I am revisiting these technologies, refreshing what I learnt and working towards a clearer understanding of how they fit together.

What I hope to develop

Through my studies at Leeds, I hope to deepen my knowledge of digital media and become more confident in putting ideas into practice. I also want to improve my English, both in academic work and in everyday communication, so that I can express my ideas more clearly and take a fuller part in discussions.

This portfolio records that process: the things I make, the questions I encounter and the connections I draw between practical work and the course materials.
